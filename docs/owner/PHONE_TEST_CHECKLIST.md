Do this on your own phone, with Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). Use **Demo** data only. Do not type real data or passwords.

## What the builder does with your answers
- Lines 1, 3, 4 close the "phone offline test" task (O1). Line 5 closes the lock-screen check (O8). Line 8 closes the prayer-time check.
- A fail is written into DECISIONS_LOG with your words. A fail on line 4 or 5 on a real phone is the evidence needed before any native (Capacitor) wrapper is considered.
- Nothing is marked Verified in the Register until you send the report.
