# Bangla plain language: pass 2 (deeper, spoken Bangla)

Status: proposal only. No source file was changed. Read-only review of `src/i18n/packs.ts` (bn pack, lines 1882-4085), `src/catalog/fields.ts`, `public/content/pack-001..009.json`, `docs/owner/WHERE_WE_ARE_BN.md`, the pass-1 decision, change log and owner note. Date: 3 Nov 2026.

Owner feedback after pass 1: the words kept as "everyday Bangla" (ব্যক্তিগত, গোনা, মাপকাঠি, বিরতি, ন্যূনতম, সর্বোচ্চ, আনুমানিক and similar) still feel bookish. He wants Bangla the way people write on a phone and in business chat: common English words in Bangla script where people really say them, simple spoken Bangla otherwise.

This file reverses the pass-1 rulings #6, #11, #12, #13, #14, #15, #16 (table 1) and adds the new words found in the full re-read (table 2). Everything else in pass 1 stands.

## How to read the tables

- `P1941` = line 1941 of `src/i18n/packs.ts` (key shown). `F262` = line 262 of `src/catalog/fields.ts`. `C3.4` = `public/content/pack-003.json`, tips[4]; `C1.34` = pack-001 tips[34]. Counts are lines in the Bangla source, not unique screens.
- "Mix" = the English form `MIX_TERMS` must get (table 10), otherwise Mix shows a Bangla word next to English ones.
- PROTECTED = the string is in the protected list of the pass-1 ruling (section 5). It appears here only when the spelling or wording around it changes, or when it is listed "no change".
- Proposed text keeps every placeholder (`{n}`, `{d}` ...) and every digit exactly.
- Numbers: Latin digits, as pass 1 decided.
