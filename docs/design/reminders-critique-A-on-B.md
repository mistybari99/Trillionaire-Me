# Critique: expert A on proposal B (written by the judge, read-only on source)

Verdict: B has the right instincts (honest premise, caps, .ics first, calm missed card) but cuts too far. It does not give the owner what he asked for.

## What B gets right (keep)
- Calendar export is the only reliable closed-app route, so it must be first-class and work with the master switch off.
- Hard cap per day, merge window, quiet hours, prayer wins collisions, count-only text for any nudge.
- Missed card auto-clears, no red, no counts of failures.
- Honest plain line in Settings about the app being closed.
- Ledger stores ids and times only, never titles.
- Do not rely on Notification Triggers or Periodic Background Sync.

## Where B fails the owner request
1. **No time choice per item.** The owner asked for a time he can choose for a task, plan, habit, bill or goal date. B offers one global lead (15/30/60) and a star. A star cannot say "remind me at 4 pm" or "the day before my bill". This is the main gap.
2. **Only items with a Placement qualify.** Bills, goal dates, habits and reviews have no Placement, so B silently excludes four of the five things the owner named.
3. **Snooze cut.** B argues iOS action buttons are unreliable. True, but snooze also lives in the in-app card and the missed card, which work everywhere. Cutting it removes the only calm way to say "not now" without marking done.
4. **A star plus "max 5 per day" is a hidden rule.** A refused sixth star is friction and a small shame moment. A cap on notifications (not on what the person may mark) is gentler.
5. **Daily nudge is closed-app unreliable too.** B sells it as a solution while it has the exact same limit as any other notification. It adds a recurring thing the owner did not ask for.
6. **"Ten ignored in a row, suggest off" needs a counter of ignores.** That is the streak-shame pattern in disguise, even if only used internally. Cut it.
7. **A new `important` field on task and plan step** changes two entities and cannot cover bills or goals. A separate reminder record is smaller overall.
8. **.ics one-shot only** with no per-reminder export: if the owner sets a 4 pm reminder he cannot put exactly that one in his calendar.

## Verdict on B's pieces
Keep: caps, quiet hours, ledger idea, test-notification button, missed card, neutral .ics, install-gate wording. Drop: star plus cap-of-5, ignored-count auto-suggest, daily nudge as the headline feature, "only Placements qualify".
