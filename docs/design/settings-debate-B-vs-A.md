## 1. Where A is better than me (I concede)

1. **Five tabs, rename More to Settings.** My 6th tab is the weakest part of B. `TABS` already has five at about 72 px each on 360 px; six is 60 px, and at 200 % text Bangla labels ("সেটিংস", "জার্নি") will wrap or clip. A's rename gives the owner the tab he asked for with no layout risk. I withdraw the 6th tab. Gear in top bar is also not needed then.
2. **Redirect table, and lock precedence.** A names a `REDIRECTS` table checked first in `render()` and notes that `isLocked()` already wins, so deep links to `/settings/*` cannot bypass the lock. B said "old routes keep working" but never said how, and never mentioned the lock. A is more precise.
3. **Master Detailed/Compact mechanics.** A spells out the one additive key (`tm:ui:view:_default`), "absent means never set", the rule that tapping a screen bar equal to the master clears the override, and a fixture test with old keys. B had the idea ("same as master = absent key") but not the clearing rule or the test. Adopt A's text.
4. **Registry plus one-home test.** `settingsRegistry.ts` with a test that every `prefs` key and every inventory row appears exactly once is a real guard for my own principle 1. B had only a prose rule. Adopt.
5. **Rows 49-51 stay on their screens with a link row in Settings.** I forced them into Settings with an allowed shortcut. A's version is simpler and keeps "see the result at once". Adopt.
6. **Name "Delete and reset" instead of "Danger zone"** for the user-facing EN label. B already avoided "বিপদ" in Bangla but kept "Danger zone" in English. Use "Delete & reset" in all three languages; "danger" stays a code word.
7. **Restore lives under Backup, not in the delete page.** I put restore in Backup too, but A states the reason better: in a panic people look for Backup. Agreed.
8. **Content links kept with unchanged URLs** and tab tag change only. Good, low churn.

## 2. Where A is wrong, risky or unbuildable

1. **A's restore confirm states something false.** A (section 4) proposes: "This replaces N records with the same ids." In `src/core/backup.ts`, `restoreBackup` does `await repo.clear(b.ns)` and then puts the file's records. It replaces the entire namespace, not "records with the same ids". Records on the phone that are not in the file are deleted. A confirm that understates the damage is worse than none. Also A has no pre-restore snapshot, no undo, and no mention that a failing `put` after `clear` leaves an empty store. A treats restore only as "add a confirm"; the real fix is preview, automatic "before restore" snapshot, atomic replace.
2. **`?hl=` anchors do not work with the current router.** `render()` takes `location.hash.replace(/^#/, "")` and matches each `ROUTES` regex against the whole string. All regexes end with `$` (for example `/^\/more\/data$/`), so `#/settings/plan?hl=automation` matches nothing and `render()` falls through to `location.hash = "#/today"`. A's redirect `/more/rules -> /settings/plan?hl=automation` and the search jump links therefore need a router change (split path and query before matching). A lists 'redirects' and 'search' as small PRs (a) and (f) but never mentions this. Buildable, but it is a core change that needs its own test.
3. **Test impact is not planned.** `grep` shows 56 occurrences of `#/more` across about 20 test files (`real_data`, `connections`, `snapshots`, `csv_*`, `o8_notify_privacy`, `a11y`, `i18n_modes`, `lang_menu`, `screen_views_more`, `m_systems_rules_ui`, and others). `nav.more` is in both language packs. A's redirects use `location.replace`, which in a test environment changes the hash and re-renders, so tests that click `[data-act]` on old routes will need rewriting. A says "every old route resolves" but not "update these 20 files in the same PR". B has the same gap (see section 4). Recommended: in the first PR keep old routes rendering the same screens, and flip them to redirects only in the PR that updates their tests.
4. **Settings home does not serve a first-time user.** A's home is quick-find box, status strip, 8 cards, then the guides group of about 13 rows. Language and text size are not on the home; they are one tap into Look. Backup is category 6 of 8. The claim "no scrolling on most phones" is not credible: at 360 px, a search box, a chip strip (4 chips wrap to two lines), 8 cards of two lines each, and a 13-row list is several screens, and at 200 % text it is many more. And `demoPanels()` renders a DEMO banner (and sometimes the delete-demo ask card) above every screen, so the first fold on Settings is partly banner (neither proposal accounts for this).
5. **Mixing settings and content in one tab again.** With the rename, Topics, Glossary, Safety, Library, Cards, Status, Field guide and Halal flags sit under "Settings". That is the same "More is a drawer" problem the audit named (problem 1), only relabelled. A mitigates with a last "Guides and info" group; I accept that, but it should be one row on the home that opens `#/settings/about`, not 13 rows on the home. Topics is already reachable from the top-bar menu button.
6. **No typed-word or equivalent extra friction for Clear all real data.** A keeps "today's two-step". Today's two-step is: tap, then tap a second button on the same card. For the only action that permanently destroys the owner's real data and (by design, `scrubRealParts`) cannot be undone from a snapshot, ask-then-confirm is easy to do on autopilot. A adds a "save a backup first" link, which is good, but friction stays at two taps.
7. **"Reset all settings" placed on the Delete page next to data deletion.** It does no data harm, so putting it beside Clear real data dilutes the one page that should hold only data deletion, and teaches users that every red-ish button there is the same. Better under Look or Guides.
8. **Consent row 10 as "switch with explanation".** A switch that turns real-data consent on in one tap, from Settings, is the opposite of D-B's "explicit tick". Consent must be granted through the shared gate (text + tick + button). A switch is acceptable only for the withdraw direction. A also does not define what withdraw does to existing real data beyond "stops saving"; reads, CSV export and backup of real data are left open.
9. **Accessibility is thin.** A defers reduce-motion and contrast, has no target-size, focus, or screen-reader order section, and did not catch the English-only `aria-label="Language"` in `langButtons()` (modeUi.ts). For a phone-only user who may not know the OS accessibility settings, a visible reduce-motion option is not scope creep. Concede that it can ship in a later PR, but it should be planned.
10. **No failure analysis.** A's risk list is engineering risk (state flags, search drift). It lacks user failure cases: JSON backup is demo-only and gives false safety, notification permission blocked while a switch shows On, lost encrypted passphrase, settings not included in backups, 200 % text, someone else holding an unlocked phone.
11. **Plan and progress as category 2.** Plan mode, stage names, progress weighting and four rules are rarely changed after setup. They are not "most often needed"; backup and reminders are. This is a judgement call, not a defect, but A gave no evidence for the ordering.

Two further decisions I treat as settled by the above:

- **Consent single source.** A and I agree on the shape: one shared `consentGate()` component used at point of use, plus a Settings > Privacy status and withdraw. Merged answer: Settings shows state, date and a **Withdraw** button only; granting always goes through the gate (tick), never a plain switch. Withdraw = stop new real writes and real export, keep existing entries read-only, show "Your N entries are still on this phone" with a link to Delete & reset. Log in `DECISIONS_LOG.md`; ask the owner one question (read-only vs hidden), default read-only.
- **Danger placement and typed word.** Last card on home, own page, page contains only Delete demo and Clear real (A's Reset settings moves out). I keep the typed word for Clear real only, but I partly concede A's "calm, no exclamation" tone: red only on the final button. **Risk I now flag in my own design:** a typed word is a real barrier for a phone-only Bangla user (keyboard switching, Unicode forms of "ডিলিট" with different composed sequences, screen-reader dictation). Answer: accept "DELETE" or "ডিলিট" after trim, NFC normalisation and case folding; test on a real Android Bangla keyboard at the G2 device test (a human task, TM-TST); if it fails, fall back to "live count + tick 'I understand N entries will be deleted' + second page + Cancel default", which is still more than today. Delete demo keeps ask-then-confirm.

## 4. Things both of us missed

1. **Router and query strings.** Neither addresses how `render()` matches sub-routes with parameters. Needed for A's search and any "jump to row". Simplest: split on `?` before matching; or avoid queries and use per-setting sub-routes.
2. **Test migration cost.** 56 `#/more` references in about 20 test files, plus tests that count nav items or read `nav.more`. Plan: keep old routes rendering real content in PR 1; convert to redirects with tests updated in the same PR; add the new registry test.
3. **DEMO banner and in-app reminder cards above Settings.** `demoPanels()` and `inAppReminders()` are inserted above every body, so Settings' real first fold is smaller than either proposal draws. Check at 360 px.
4. **Settings are not in backups.** `tm:ui:*` is localStorage; `createBackup`/snapshots handle records. After restore or a browser data clear the phone looks reset. Say it in Backup, and decide later whether non-sensitive look settings join the file.
5. **i18n cost.** Every new label/hint is a key in the EN pack, the BN pack and Mix; tests (`i18n_modes`, `latin_digits`, `copy_review`, `bd_pack`, `content_pack`) check keys and wording. Budget for about 150 new strings, loanword rule applied, all still "যাচাই হয়নি".
6. **Lazy loading and offline.** `viaLazy` exists for heavy screens. The Settings pages should be a lazy chunk (only Look/Privacy needed at first paint) and be in the service worker precache so Settings works offline on first visit.
7. **Namespace behaviour of settings.** `prayerCoords` is per namespace and cleared by Clear real data; Settings must show which namespace a place belongs to. A noted it, B too, but neither put it in the tests.
8. **One-time "what moved" note for the owner.** He knows the old More screen. A one-line dismissible card "Settings and Guides are now here" prevents "my buttons disappeared".
9. **Register mapping.** Neither says which TM-REQ/TM-TST rows this block touches or adds. Check the Register for existing settings/privacy requirements and link tests; do not mark Verified without artifacts.
10. **Version text and storage usage.** An "About this app" line (version, schema version, space used) helps support and the owner; not in either list. Low priority.
11. **Dictation and voice use.** A phone-only owner may use voice input. Labels should be short and unique so voice-control ("tap Backup") works; neither proposal checked label uniqueness.

## 5. Short verdict

A is the better navigation and migration plan (tab rename, redirects, registry test, master density). B is the better safety, privacy and accessibility plan (restore, consent withdraw semantics, live private-by-default, failure cases). The merged answer is A's skeleton with B's restore flow, B's inline basics on the home, and B's failure checklist. A's single factual error (what restore replaces) must be fixed before anything is built.
